# Growth report guidance

Use first-ever received donation, not CRM record creation, for Growth. Clearly label synthetic results. Include the period, target, actual, target attainment, shortfall, comparison period and exclusions. Explain that complete lifetime history is required.

Use the Brand MCP's document guidance and assets when producing a branded document. Preserve the calculated values and evidence references. Do not invent causes for changes in donor acquisition.

This document controls reporting guidance only. Counting rules are implemented and tested in model.mjs; changing this text cannot change those rules.
